⚠️
Current problem: A single lengthy form (30+ fields) is presented to every user regardless of their role or environment type. A CxO wanting a sandbox must fill the same form as a Cloud Architect designing a production workload — creating friction, errors, and drop-off.
Design Principles
🎯
Right Person, Right Time
Technical details are gathered from the person who actually knows them — Dev team for Dev, EA team for Production. Not the business requester.
⬅️
Shift Left
Sandbox and Dev environments start immediately — no approval gates. Work begins in parallel with governance, not after it.
🤖
AI Does the Heavy Lifting
Cloud Architecture Blueprint is AI-generated from structured inputs. No manual document authoring. LOB auto-fills all billing metadata.

Before vs After
❌ Current State
Single 30+ field form for every user and every environment
CxO / Business user must fill technical architecture details they don't know
EA approval required even for sandbox and dev — blocking work from starting
Sandbox and Production follow identical, heavyweight governance paths
10+ manual billing fields required from the requester
Nothing happens until every phase completes sequentially
✅ Proposed State
3 tailored flows — form complexity matches the environment and persona
CxO fills 5–9 business fields only. Technical detail gathered by Architect / Dev team
Sandbox & Dev bypass EA completely — provisioned and live immediately
Production gets full EA enrichment with CAB generated from expert inputs
2 fields (Company + LOB) auto-populate all billing metadata
Dev team works in sandbox while governance runs in parallel for Production
